Privacy Policy

ilmejafar.com
Last updated: 2026

At ilmejafar.com, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this website, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require you to create an account to use any tool on this site. We do not ask for your email address, phone number, government ID, or any payment information. There is no login system, and we do not build user profiles.

2. Information You Voluntarily Provide

When you use one of our tools — such as Ilm-e-Jafar Akhbar, Ilm-e-Jafar Aasar, Ism-e-Azam, name compatibility, or baby name guidance — you may voluntarily enter information such as your name, your mother's name, a day of the week, or a written question. This information is used solely to generate the requested spiritual analysis and is sent securely to our backend service, which in turn communicates with an AI language model to produce your result.

We do not permanently store the names or questions you enter for the purpose of building a profile about you. This data is processed transiently to generate your result and is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

This website uses your browser's local storage (not traditional tracking cookies) for two purposes only:

  • To remember your selected language (Urdu, English, Hindi, or Arabic) so you don't need to reselect it on every visit.
  • To track daily usage counts for each tool on your specific device, so we can apply a fair daily usage limit and keep the service free and available for everyone.

This data stays on your own device and is not transmitted to us or to any third party as a means of tracking your identity across the web.

4. AI Processing of Your Questions

To generate the detailed spiritual interpretation for your question, the text you provide is sent to an AI language model via our backend API. This is necessary to produce a meaningful, personalized response rather than a generic, pre-written answer. We instruct this AI service to respond only within the traditional framework of Ilm-e-Jafar, in the language you have selected, and we do not use this data to train any AI model ourselves.

5. Google AdSense and Advertising

ilmejafar.com displays advertisements through Google AdSense to help keep the site free for all users. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data, and how to opt out of personalized advertising, by visiting Google's Advertising Policy page and Google Ads Settings.

6. Third-Party Services We Use

We rely on a small number of trusted third-party services to operate this website:

  • AI language model provider — processes the text of your questions to generate spiritual analyses. This provider has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data sent to its API.
  • Google AdSense — displays advertisements and may collect data as described above.
  • Formspree — powers our Contact page's message form. Messages sent through this form are delivered securely to our email inbox and are not shared with anyone else. You can review Formspree's Privacy Policy for further detail.

7. Data Retention

Names and questions submitted through our spiritual guidance tools are processed to generate your result and are not retained afterward for profiling purposes. Messages submitted through our Contact form are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to review and respond to your inquiry.

8. Children's Privacy

ilmejafar.com is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. Your Choices

Since we do not maintain user accounts, there is no personal profile to request, edit, or delete on our end. You can clear your browser's local storage at any time to reset your saved language preference and daily usage counters. You can also adjust your browser or device settings to limit cookies from advertising partners like Google.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect the information transmitted through this site, including the use of secure HTTPS connections. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10a. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal that you can enable in your settings. There is currently no universal industry standard for how websites should respond to DNT signals, and ilmejafar.com does not currently change its behavior in response to DNT signals. However, because we do not build long-term user profiles in the first place, the practical tracking exposure on our site remains limited regardless of this setting.

11. International Users

ilmejafar.com is used by visitors from many countries, including Pakistan, India, and across the Middle East. By using this site, you understand that your information may be processed by our service providers in locations outside your own country, in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

12. Your Regional Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have specific legal rights regarding your personal data. For example, residents of the European Economic Area may have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and residents of California may have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what information is collected about you and the right to request its deletion. Because ilmejafar.com does not require account creation and does not retain personal profiles, most of these requests are naturally satisfied by our data-minimal approach — but if you have specific concerns, please reach out through our Contact page and we will do our best to assist.

13. Managing Cookies in Your Browser

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can typically choose to block all cookies, block only third-party cookies, or be notified whenever a cookie is set. Please note that blocking all cookies, including local storage, may mean that features like language preference and daily usage tracking on ilmejafar.com will not work as expected, and you may need to reselect your language each visit. Instructions for managing cookies can usually be found in your browser's "Settings" or "Privacy" menu.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, regulatory, or operational reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

15. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please reach out through our Contact page.
